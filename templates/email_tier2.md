# Email Template — Tier 2 (Medium-Relevance Prospects)
**Strategy: Content Gift + Social Proof**

Tier 2 contacts are a moderate fit — they work in your general field but may not specialize in your exact topic. Lead with a free, useful resource. Keep it short and low-pressure.

---

## Placeholders used

| Placeholder | Source | Example |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| `[ORG_NAME]` | `org_name` field | "Community Skills Centre" |
| `[PROJECT_NAME]` | `project_name` field | "Digital Futures" |
| `[RELEVANT_THEME]` | AI-extracted by step5 | "digital upskilling for the workforce" |
| `[YOUR_NAME]` | Your name from config | "Alex" |
| `[YOUR_ORG]` | Your org from config | "Acme Org" |
| `[YOUR_WEBSITE]` | Your website from config | "acme.org" |
| `[YOUR_EMAIL]` | Your email from config | "alex@acme.org" |
| `[FREE_RESOURCE]` | Your free offer | "Session 1 lesson plan" |
| `[PORTFOLIO_LINK]` | Tracked link to your portfolio | auto-inserted |

---

## Initial Email

**Subject:** Quick idea for [ORG_NAME] — [your topic]

Hi,

Your [PROJECT_NAME] project caught my eye — the focus on [RELEVANT_THEME] connects directly to something we've built at [YOUR_ORG] ([YOUR_WEBSITE]).

[Describe your offering in 2 sentences. What is it? What outcome does it produce?]

[Include a link to a live example: [PORTFOLIO_LINK]]

I'd love to send you a free copy of [FREE_RESOURCE] — takes 2 minutes to review. If it fits what [ORG_NAME] does, I'd be happy to share more.

Interested?

[YOUR_NAME]
[YOUR_ORG] | [YOUR_WEBSITE]

---

## Follow-up 1 (Day 3)

**Subject:** Re: Quick idea for [ORG_NAME] — [your topic]

Hi,

Quick follow-up — would the free [FREE_RESOURCE] be useful for your team?

[One sentence on what's included and why it's valuable.]

Just say the word and I'll send it over.

[YOUR_NAME] | [YOUR_ORG] | [YOUR_EMAIL]

---

## Follow-up 2 (Day 7 — breakup)

**Subject:** Re: Quick idea for [ORG_NAME] — [your topic]

Hi,

Last follow-up from me. If [YOUR_OFFER] doesn't fit right now, no worries at all.

If timing is better later in the year, feel free to reach out anytime.

[YOUR_NAME] | [YOUR_EMAIL] | [YOUR_WEBSITE]

---

## If They Respond Positively → Transition to Pilot

Once they express interest, offer a no-commitment pilot:

> "We're currently looking for 2-3 organisations to run a free pilot of [FREE_RESOURCE] with one of your cohorts. You'd get full materials and support from our side. In return, we'd love your honest feedback on how it landed.
>
> This could also be a building block for future collaboration, if that's something [ORG_NAME] would consider."
